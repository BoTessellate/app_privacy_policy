Common Questions

  • How does a fit check work? Set up your body profile (height and proportions), then paste a clothing product link. Alate returns a fit score and specific warnings tailored to you.
  • A brand or link didn't work. Some stores can't be read yet. On the result screen you can tap "notify me" to be told when that brand is added.
  • Do I need an account? No. Core fit checks work with no sign-in. Google sign-in is optional and only syncs your profile across devices.
  • Where is my data? Your body profile and fit history stay on your device by default. See the links below.
